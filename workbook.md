## How to use this workbook
Read each module, change **one synthetic signal parameter at a time** in the browser lab, then record the difference between the clean reference and the observed trace. All charts are illustrative and cannot predict real-world jamming effectiveness. After each module, complete a randomized five-question knowledge check from a pool of 50 question variants; passing score is **4/5**.

The full worksheet is also printable from the **Workbook** tab of the web application. Use the prompts below as a reusable study guide.

## Instructor / student guidance

- Use the module-specific ten concepts shown in the web app as a vocabulary checklist.
- Save notebook observations locally in your browser or write them above.
- Browser storage is device-specific; it is not cloud-synced.
- Avoid treating illustrative signal contrast numbers as calibrated signal-to-noise or jamming calculations.
- No transmit hardware, radio control, or real-world interference operations are provided.

**Final reflection:** Distinguish what a waveform observation demonstrates from what you would still need to measure or verify.
